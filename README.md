# Globe System (GDS)

**A complete multi-layer domain framework for understanding everything on Earth.**

Created collaboratively.

---

## What is Globe System?

Globe System (GDS) is a structured map of reality that organises the entire globe into clear layers and domains:

- **Layer 1** — Foundational Reality (Natural + Biological)
- **Layer 2** — Human Organisation (Cultural & Societal)
- **Layer 3** — Interaction Zone (where humans and nature constantly affect each other)

It is designed to help people see the whole picture, find connections, compare places or times, and think systemically.

## How the Layers Connect

- **Layer 1** sets the physical and biological limits of what is possible.
- **Layer 2** is everything humans organise and create on top of Layer 1.
- **Layer 3** is the continuous feedback loop — human activity changes nature, and nature forces humans to adapt.

Everything is interconnected. A change in one domain eventually affects others.

---

## What GDS Can Be Used For

- Mapping any place, culture, country, or community completely
- Seeing hidden connections between domains
- Comparing different societies or time periods
- Diagnosing strengths, weaknesses, and gaps
- Supporting research, strategy, education, and systems thinking
- Organising knowledge about the world
